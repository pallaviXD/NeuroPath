LAYER 2 — ADAPTIVE CONTENT ENGINE

One curriculum. Four completely different lessons.

The same "photosynthesis" lesson becomes a diagram, a story, a builder, or a signed walkthrough — generated per learner from one underlying curriculum, not four separate courses.

📊
Visual
Animated, colour-coded diagrams. Information laid out spatially so relationships are seen, not just read.
e.g. chloroplast diagram with glowing energy-flow arrows
📖
Narrative
The concept told as a story with characters and stakes — a leaf's day, an electron's journey home.
e.g. "A Day in the Life of a Leaf" personified story
🧪
Kinesthetic
Drag-and-drop, build-it-yourself interactions. Understanding through doing, not reading about doing.
e.g. drag-and-drop molecule builder
🤟
Sign Language
A 3D signing avatar delivers the full explanation inline — not a caption, not a bolted-on accessibility mode.
e.g. signed walkthrough, generated on the fly
LAYER 3 — STRUGGLE DETECTION + MICRO-INTERVENTION

Re-reading the same line, a long pause, a wrong answer followed by a self-correction — NeuroPath reads these the way a great human tutor does, then tries a different angle before the student even asks for help.
